What we do

Three skills. One clean finish.

Most jobs that come through the shop need all three. Pick one to see what it covers.

Mild steel · Stainless steel

Strong, even seams on sheet and plate, laid by hand with attention to heat so thin metal does not warp or burn through.

  • Butt, lap, corner and fillet joints
  • Tack and continuous seams
  • Low spatter and tidy beads, so little clean-up
  • Repair welds on cracked or broken parts
Drawing, sketch or sample

Cutting, bending and fitting sheet into boxes, panels, frames and brackets, as one-off pieces or in batches.

  • Enclosures, covers, hoods and trays
  • Frames, stands and supports
  • Built from a drawing, a sketch, a photo or an old part
  • One piece or repeat batches
Flush, smooth, paint-ready

The step that turns a rough weld into a finished part. Seams are ground flush and edges are made safe to handle.

  • Weld seams ground and blended flush
  • Burrs removed and sharp edges rounded
  • Surface prepared for paint, powder coat or plating
  • Even finish on every visible joint

How a job runs

From drawing to finished part

Five steps. You know what happens at each one.

1

Share the job

Send a drawing, a sketch, a photo or a sample piece. Tell us the size, material, thickness and how many you need.

2

Get the quote

We check the details, confirm what is needed and give you a clear price and timeline before any work starts.

3

Cut, bend and fit

Sheet is cut and formed to size, then clamped and tack-fitted so every part lines up before the real welding begins.

4

Weld and grind

MIG welding joins the parts. Visible seams are then ground and blended, edges are broken and the surface is left ready for paint or coating.

5

Check and hand over

The finished piece is checked against your drawing or sample, then it is ready for you to collect. Ask us about delivery.

Questions

Before you ask

What can I send for a quote?

A drawing, a hand sketch, a clear photo or a sample piece. Tell us the size, material and how many you need. We will come back with a price and a timeline.

Which materials do you work with?

Mild steel and stainless steel sheet are our everyday materials. If you have something else in mind, ask us. We will tell you plainly whether we can do it well.

Do you take small orders?

Yes. A single repair or a one-off part is welcome, and so are repeat batches.

Can you copy an existing part?

Bring the old part, or a clear photo with measurements, and we will reproduce it or improve it.

How long will my job take?

It depends on the size, the quantity and the material. We confirm the timeline with the quote. If it is urgent, say so when you ask.
